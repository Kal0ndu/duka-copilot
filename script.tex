\section*{Recording Notes}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pacing:} Aim for a relaxed, conversational
    delivery. Let important figures and recommendations
    register with the viewer.

    \item \textbf{Timing:} Follow the timestamps as closely
    as possible. If a section runs long, remove its second
    sentence before accelerating the narration.

    \item \textbf{Screen directions:} These are production
    cues only; do not read them aloud.

    \item \textbf{Audio alignment:} Record while watching
    the video, or insert approximately 0.5 seconds of silence
    before the first spoken word to prevent clipping.

    \item \textbf{Closing:} Deliver the final tagline clearly,
    then leave a brief pause at the end.
\end{itemize}
